Neuromatch Academy: Computational Neuroscience

Computational neuroscience

Neuromatch Academy

Highly recommended for prospective Ph.D. students and undergraduate researchers before joining the lab. Work through the coding exercises alongside the lectures. Focus on models of neurons and populations, dimensionality reduction, dynamical systems, and learning.

Getting started

BookFoundational reading

Cognitive Psychology: A Student’s Handbook

Foundations of cognition

Michael W. Eysenck & Mark T. Keane (2020) · 8th edition · Routledge

DOI: 10.4324/9781351058513

A starting point for the cognitive questions behind our research. Suggested focus: working memory and executive attention (cognitive control); learning and forgetting; problem solving, judgment, and reasoning. Relate the behavioral findings to the neural mechanisms discussed in the papers below.

Getting started / Books / Working memory

PaperFoundational reading

The Neural Representation of Sequences: From Transition Probabilities to Algebraic Patterns and Linguistic Trees

Representing sequences

Dehaene, Meyniel, Wacongne, Wang & Pallier (2015) · Neuron

DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2015.09.019

Stanislas Dehaene and colleagues outline levels of sequence representation, from transition statistics and chunks to abstract rules and nested structures. Use this review to frame questions about compositional representations.

Getting started / Sequence

PaperFoundational reading

Geometry of sequence working memory in macaque prefrontal cortex

Sequence working memory

Xie, Y., Hu, P., et al. (2022) · Science

DOI: 10.1126/science.abm0204

Read Xie, Hu and colleagues for a concrete example of neural population geometry in primate cognition. Focus on how item identity and ordinal position are represented and how the population analyses support the conclusions.

Getting started / Sequence / Paper Pool / Journal Club

PaperFoundational reading

Opening the Black Box: Low-Dimensional Dynamics in High-Dimensional Recurrent Neural Networks

Understanding neural dynamics

Sussillo & Barak (2013) · Neural Computation

DOI: 10.1162/NECO_a_00409

Learn how fixed points and local linearization can reveal the computations of a trained recurrent neural network. Connect the dynamical-systems viewpoint to the population geometry in the sequence-working-memory paper.

Getting started / NeuroAI & computation

BookFoundational reading

An Introduction to Statistical Learning: with Applications in Python

Learning algorithms · supervised & unsupervised

Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani & Jonathan Taylor (2023) · Springer

DOI: 10.1007/978-3-031-38747-0

Start with statistical learning, regression, classification, and resampling. Then study regularization, neural networks, PCA, and clustering. For neural networks, connect loss functions, gradient descent, and backpropagation. Use the Python labs to practice fitting a model, separating training from test data, and evaluating generalization. This introduces supervised and unsupervised learning before more specialized neural models.

Getting started / Books / NeuroAI & computation

BookFoundational reading

Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction

Learning algorithms · reinforcement learning

Richard S. Sutton & Andrew G. Barto (2018) · 2nd edition · MIT Press

Begin with bandit problems and Markov decision processes, then compare dynamic programming, Monte Carlo methods, and temporal-difference learning. Work through value updates and the exploration–exploitation trade-off. Use these ideas to connect learning algorithms with reward, choice, and adaptive behavior.
