Join Xie Lab

Interested in how the brain learns, remembers, and reasons? We welcome inquiries from prospective postdoctoral researchers, Ph.D. students, and undergraduates.

Postdoctoral researchers

We welcome candidates interested in studying cognition using electrophysiology, imaging, or single-cell optogenetics. Experience with these techniques and a strong background in data analysis and computational modeling are desired. The lab is based in Minhang, Shanghai. Please email your CV.

Ph.D. students

Students interested in systems neuroscience or NeuroAI are warmly welcome. We recruit students with backgrounds in physics, mathematics, biology, computer science, or psychology. Please email your CV and a research statement. We highly recommend completing Neuromatch Academy’s Computational Neuroscience course before joining the lab.

Undergraduate researchers

We welcome students with backgrounds in physics, mathematics, biology, computer science, or psychology. If you are interested in joining, please email us, preferably with your CV or a personal statement. We highly recommend completing Neuromatch Academy’s Computational Neuroscience course before joining the lab.
